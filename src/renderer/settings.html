          Signing in on your devices

          Every time the phone app or her page is opened, it asks for this name and password. They are set and changed only here, on this PC. Changing them signs every device out.

          External MCP tools

          Connect local or private-LAN Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers (such as Home Assistant, Obsidian, or local developer tools). Every tool asks your permission by default, and all MCP responses are treated as untrusted outside text.

            Your other machines

            A server or a NAS she may work on for you over SSH. She gets a key of her own for each one; you let it in once. Every command is shown to you before it runs, except the plain status checks on machines where you tick the box.

              Is this the machine? Compare with the machine's own answer. On it, run: for f in /etc/ssh/ssh_host_*_key.pub; do ssh-keygen -lf "$f"; done

              Her memory (100% local & editable)

              You own every note she remembers — stored strictly on this computer in plain JSON, never uploaded to any cloud. Pin key facts to keep them at the top of her prompt, correct any mistake inline, forget anything, or click Why? to see where a memory came from.
