          Signing in on your devices

          Every time the phone app or her page is opened, it asks for this name and password. They are set and changed only here, on this PC. Changing them signs every device out.

          Your other machines

          A server or a NAS she may work on for you over SSH. She gets a key of her own for each one; you let it in once. Every command is shown to you before it runs, except the plain status checks on machines where you tick the box.
